### [MewTools](https://github.com/MewDMA/MewTools)

Free app for your second PC. Works with any 35T, 75T or 100T card, not just ours. Check your card, grab your DNA ID, install drivers, flash firmware with chip checks, set up MAKCU and FERRUM, and get a fresh Windows ready. Every change can be undone.
